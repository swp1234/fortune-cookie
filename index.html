Frequently Asked Questions

Where do fortune cookies originate historically?

While popularized in early 20th-century California Chinese restaurants, their culinary precursors trace to 19th-century Japanese temple confectioneries known as tsujiura senbei, which folded paper fortunes into roasted wafers.

Why do fortune cookie messages feel so resonant and personal?

This feeling stems from psychological priming and the Barnum-Forer effect. Broad, optimistic statements encourage your mind to identify relevant personal circumstances and positive opportunities in daily life.

How should daily fortunes and lucky numbers be used?

Fortune cookies are cultural entertainment and mindfulness prompts. Rather than literal predictions or gambling advice, use them for positive reflection, daily motivation, and creative inspiration.

Is my fortune opening history tracked or saved remotely?

No. All cookie cracking animations, daily counters, and selections run entirely in your local browser. No personal data or fortune logs are transmitted to external servers.
